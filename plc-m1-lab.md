# Craft an Advanced Product Strategy, Module 1 Lab

## Write your one-page strategy
- **Winning aspiration: define winning in the customer's terms, not internal metrics. What does success look like for the specific person you serve?:** Become the app people continue to rely on after a difficult period -helping them maintain calm, resilience, and emotional balance as part of everyday life.
- **Where to play: segment, geography, and use case, explicitly. Which customers, markets, and surfaces will you focus on?:** Existing highly engaged Fable users aged 25-45 transitioning out of acute stress, especially premium users approaching the 60-120 day window when disengagement typically begins.
- **How to win: your differentiator. What can you do that your specific competitors cannot easily replicate?:** Make Fable part of users’ everyday wellbeing by evolving the experience as their needs change, helping them stay consistent, discover relevant wellness insights, and connect with others.
- **Capabilities required: what you must be world-class at. What will you build, buy, or partner for?:** Build four connected experiences: evolving exercises and routines; reminders and regular wellbeing check-ins; personalized wellness content, research, and advice based on users’ interests; and community experiences that support motivation and connection.
- **Management systems: the metrics and rituals that keep your choices alive quarter to quarter.:** Track post-90-day retention, premium renewal, weekly meaningful engagement, progression into post-acute journeys, and personalized recommendation adoption; review cohort retention and experiment results monthly.

## Name your one hard no
- **Your one hard no:** We will not become a clinical or therapy platform. We will focus on everyday wellbeing and helping users sustain healthy habits beyond the acute phase.

## Write your three OKRs
- **Objective:** Make Fable a lasting part of users’ everyday wellbeing beyond the acute phase
- **KR1:** Reduce post-90-day churn among highly engaged users by 20%.
- **KR2:** Increase the share of post-90-day users who engage with Fable at least 3 days per week from baseline to 40%
- **KR3:** Increase premium renewal among users reaching the post-acute phase from baseline to +15%

## AI pressure-test
- **Which challenge from the AI is most valid, and why?:** The biggest assumption is that users who no longer need Fable for acute stress actually want Fable to remain part of their everyday lives
- **What would you change based on the pushback, and what would you defend?:** Create a wellness journey that evolves with users not after from day one - adapting the experience as they move from immediate relief to building habits, maintaining wellbeing, and strengthening resilience.
