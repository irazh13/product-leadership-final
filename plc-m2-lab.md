# Prioritization & Roadmapping, Module 2 Lab

## Prioritize your Rocks and your Hard Nos
- **Rock #1:** Redesign onboarding flow based on 40% drop-off data. 

Impact: 5 / Effort: 2. The evolving Fable journey starts on Day 1. It directly supports the new strategy while addressing the proven 40% onboarding drop-off.
- **Rock #2:** Add a daily streak feature to drive habit formation

Impact: 4 / Effort: 2. A relatively low-effort way to encourage consistency and help users turn Fable from an acute-stress tool into an ongoing wellbeing habit.
- **Rock #3:** Fix the notification system (wrong times, timezone bug)

Timely reminders are essential for building consistent routines. Fixing the existing system enables the strategy without requiring a major new capability.
- **❌ Hard No #1:** Launch a premium tier with therapist-matching 

Impact: 1 / Effort: 5. Moves Fable toward a clinical/therapy model, directly conflicting with our strategic and regulatory boundaries.
- **❌ Hard No #2:** Introduce "Fable for Teams", a B2B workplace wellbeing play

Impact: 2 / Effort: 5. Requires significant investment with no evidence that web access will improve post-acute retention or our core growth OKRs
- **❌ Hard No #3:** Build a web version (frequently requested in reviews)

Impact: 1 / Effort: 5. Introduces a new B2B customer, buyer, product motion, and GTM model without addressing the current consumer retention problem.
